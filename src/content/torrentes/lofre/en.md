## Overview
The L'Ofre Torrent is one of the most spectacular and sporty vertical descents in the Serra de Tramuntana. It rises beneath the pyramid-shaped Puig de l'Ofre and plunges down toward the iconic Barranc de Biniaraix, offering continuous verticals, airy rappels, and panoramic views over the Sóller valley.

## Approach
- **From Cúber (usual route):** Park at the Cúber reservoir, follow the GR-221 trail around the reservoir, and climb to the Coll de l'Ofre. From the pass, descend south toward the start of the torrent's gully.
- **From Biniaraix:** Hike up the cobbled path of the Barranc de Biniaraix to the Coll de l'Ofre (physically demanding).

## Descent and key sections
- **Initial narrows:** A quick succession of rappels of 15m to 25m over polished rock.
- **The Great Vertical (42m rappel):** An impressive airy rappel that requires good rope management and friction control.
- **Final section:** A continuous descent with technical drops between boulders down to the cobbled path.

## Return
After the last rappel, the torrent crosses the cobbled trail of the Barranc de Biniaraix (GR-221). Simply follow the cobbled path downhill to the village of Biniaraix.
