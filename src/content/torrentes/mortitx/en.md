## Overview
The Fondo de Mortitx Torrent is one of Mallorca's great classic descents. It's a narrow canyon carved from beautifully sculpted limestone that flows directly into the sea at Cala Mortitx. Although it has no major vertical difficulties, its deep pools, slippery ledges, and the technical nature of its exit make it a demanding route.

## Approach
The route starts at the Mortitx winery car park (km 10.9 of the Ma-10 road between Pollença and Lluc). Follow the forest track past the vineyards and the Mortitx refuge, heading into the karst valley until you reach the dry streambed near the Bec d'Oca.

## Descent and key sections
- **First section:** Open gorges with small drops and easy downclimbs.
- **Narrow section:** The canyon narrows sharply, with several consecutive rappels between 8m and 20m.
- **Pools and siphons:** Several possible jumps (always check the depth first) and cold, enclosed pools.
- **Final rappel:** An airy descent down to the sea cove where the torrent meets the Mediterranean.

## Return
- **On foot (Pas de s'Estaca):** Demanding and exposed. Exit along the right side of the cove, crossing chains, staples, and loose terrain in a dramatic coastal setting, before crossing the karst to rejoin the Mortitx track.
- **By sea:** Arrange a boat pickup toward Cala Sant Vicenç or Port de Pollença in advance (recommended in poor conditions or for large groups).
