## Overview
Considered **the most iconic and technical canyon in Europe**, Sa Fosca is a colossal limestone gorge whose walls close in to more than 300 metres high, plunging canyoneers into total darkness for over a kilometre of underground passage. It is a top-level challenge in route-finding, physical endurance, and mental composure.

## Critical warnings
- **Lighting:** A dual waterproof lighting system per person, with spare batteries, is essential.
- **No escape route:** Once you enter the dark section, there are no intermediate exits until the Entreforc.
- **Water flow:** Entry is forbidden if storms are forecast or after torrential rain, due to the risk of blockage and drowning in the siphons.

## Descent and key sections
- **Open section (Gorg Blau):** Cold-water pools, drops, and the first rappels in open air.
- **Entrance to Sa Fosca (El Pont Natural):** The rock progressively closes in until it fades into dusk and then total darkness.
- **Dark section:** More than 25 rappels, slippery downclimbs, siphons, and a chaos of boulders lit only by headlamps.
- **Exit at the Entreforc:** Daylight reappears at the majestic confluence with the Torrent de Lluc, forming the Torrent de Pareis.

## Return
- **Usual option:** Climb out from the Entreforc via the steep **Pas des Ruc** trail / Escorca houses to the Ma-10 road (1h 30min of steep ascent).
- **Pareis option:** Continue down the Torrent de Pareis to its mouth at Sa Calobra (adds 4-5 extra hours).
