## Overview
Located in the beautiful Coanegra valley, this torrent is famous for its opening waterfall, the **Salt des Freu**, a stepped cascade of tufa limestone surrounded by holm oak forest. It's one of the island's most popular descents when running with water after the autumn and winter storms, and it's playful and accessible.

## Approach
- **Two-car option:** Leave one car at the start of the Camí de Coanegra (Santa Maria) and drive the other up to the Orient road (around km 8.5). From the access gate, walk 15-20 minutes along the marked trail to the Salt des Freu.
- **Circular option (one car in Orient):** Descend the torrent and walk back up via the Coanegra valley trail.

## Descent and key sections
- **Salt des Freu (R1 and R2):** The main staged waterfall (up to 24m), spectacular when carrying water.
- **Middle section:** A chain of small slides, pools, and rappels of 6m to 15m over tufa formations.
- **Exit:** The canyon gradually opens up toward the valley trail, making it easy to leave the streambed.

## Return
After the last rappel, follow the clear trail running alongside the streambed on the right bank down to the houses of Son Roig / Camí de Coanegra.
